# AI-Assisted Analytics Integration

**Area:** API integration, business analytics, AI-assisted reporting  
**Status:** Working integration, with ongoing use and evaluation

## Business need

Comparing website performance across multiple business locations can involve switching between separate analytics views and manually compiling traffic and engagement information.

## Approach

Connected ChatGPT to Google Analytics 4 reporting for two LearningRx locations, enabling conversational retrieval and comparison of website performance information.

Workflows include reviewing traffic, acquisition channels, engagement, and performance trends to support business decisions.

## Engineering considerations

- Consistent metrics and comparable reporting periods across locations
- Clear identification of the data source and reporting window
- Handling unavailable metrics without presenting invented values
- Keeping business-specific data and access credentials private

## Scope and evidence

The ChatGPT integrations have been used to retrieve data for both centers. This document does **not** claim a custom-built GA4 MCP server, an independently audited analytics pipeline, or a public API implementation.

## Skills illustrated

API integrations, systems integration, Google Analytics, business reporting, and AI-assisted analysis.
