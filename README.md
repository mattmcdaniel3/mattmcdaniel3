# Matt McDaniel
### Software Engineering Leadership | Business Automation | AI Integration

I bring a background in enterprise software development and engineering leadership to practical business automation and AI-assisted development.

I spent 19 years at Worldpay, progressing from software engineering into senior application development leadership. My teams supported mission-critical payment systems, including merchant chargebacks and disputes, ACH and wire funding, debit-network processes, and gift-card applications.

Today, I work on business systems, operations, integrations, and automation supporting two LearningRx centers in the Atlanta area.

## What I work on

- **Business process automation:** Reducing repetitive steps and improving the reliability of routine workflows.
- **Systems integration:** Connecting business applications, calendars, email systems, analytics, and reporting.
- **AI-assisted software development:** Applying AI coding tools alongside testing, review, and operational safeguards.
- **Data and reporting:** Making business information easier to retrieve, validate, and interpret.
- **AI tool integration:** Exploring API- and Model Context Protocol (MCP)-based approaches to connecting assistants with existing systems.
